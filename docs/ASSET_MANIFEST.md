# Asset manifest

Generated from content by `AssetManifest` (menu Hollowcrawl > Export Asset Manifest, or the
`HOLLOWCRAWL_WRITE_MANIFEST=1 dotnet test Tests/CoreTests --filter AssetManifest` test). Do not edit by hand.

Drop files at these paths; anything missing falls back to a generated shape or silence:

- Sprites: `Assets/Resources/Sprites/<key>.png` pixel art at 32 px per world unit, pivot centre; a horizontal strip of square frames animates, and `<key>_move.png` is the walk cycle).
- Tiles: `Assets/Resources/Tiles/<theme>/<kind>.png` or `Assets/Resources/Tiles/<kind>.png`, 32x32 pixel art (a horizontal strip holds variants), Read/Write enabled.
- Most of these are procedural pixel art generated by `Tools/PixelArt` (see `docs/ART_PIPELINE.md`).
- Audio: `Assets/Resources/Audio/<key>.ogg` (music loops; effects one-shot).
